📖 Detailed Quantitative Methodology & Root-Cause Diagnostic (v2.5)

1. Why Previous Scans Rejected 41 / 41 Stocks (Diagnostic Audit)

  • Reversed V3 Candle Chronology Bug: Upstox /v3/historical-candle returns newest candles at index 0. Computing 5-day Z-scores without reversing the array flipped the sign of Z5D (showing +0.40 to +1.59 on oversold pullbacks like ABB, DRREDDY, SIEMENS, TITAN). Because the filter required Z5D ≤ -1.25, 40 of 41 stocks failed immediately.
  • Synthetic Fractional Strikes & Negative RoR% Bug: Multiplying the short strike by 0.96 created non-existent fractional hedge strikes (e.g., 1881.6 PE) and subtracted a hardcoded ₹7.00 credit from a ₹5.80 spread width on TATASTEEL and WIPRO, producing -583.3% RoR.
  • Inverted POP% Bug on Live Options: On stocks where Upstox returned option Greeks (HDFCBANK, INFY, SBIN), the table showed 22% POP because it displayed $|\Delta|$ (Probability of ITM) rather than $1 - |\Delta|$ (Probability of Expiring Worthless).

2. Core Mathematical Engine: Probability of Expiring Worthless (P_worthless)

Our single overriding objective is to sell OTM Put options (or Bull Put Credit Spreads) that expire worthless (S_T ≥ K_short) while harvesting overpriced Implied Volatility. Instead of trusting noisy broker POP fields, we compute the empirical lognormal survival probability N(d2) using the 20-day Yang-Zhang (2000) minimum-variance OHLC volatility estimator (σ_YZ):

σ²_YZ = σ²_overnight + k · σ²_open_to_close + (1 - k) · σ²_Rogers_Satchell
d₂ = [ ln(Spot / K_short) + (r - 0.5 · σ²_YZ) · T ] / ( σ_YZ · √T )
Empirical POP (%) = Φ(d₂) × 100   (Cross-checked with 100 - |Δ_short| × 100)

3. The 5 Calibrated Pillars (Not Too Strict, Not Too Lenient)

  • Pillar I — Empirical POP & Strike Safety Cushion: Short Put strike K_short is selected from actual NSE listed strikes in the -0.26 to -0.14 Delta zone at or below the highest Put OI Support Wall, requiring Empirical POP ≥ 76% and an OTM safety cushion (S - K_short)/S ≥ 5.0%.
  • Pillar II — Structural Floor & Uptrend Integrity: Requires Spot / 200-DMA ≥ 0.92x. This keeps secular compounders experiencing normal pullbacks (like SUNPHARMA at 0.97x, CIPLA at 0.97x, GRASIM at 0.98x, DRREDDY at 0.99x) while rejecting broken secular downtrends (GODREJCP at 0.81x, VOLTAS at 0.78x, MARUTI at 0.84x).
  • Pillar III — Exhaustion / Non-Blowoff Regime: Replaces the ultra-rare RSI(2) ≤ 22 AND Z5D ≤ -1.25 lock with a balanced regime guard: RSI(14) ≤ 60 (or RSI(2) ≤ 50) and -2.60 ≤ Z5D ≤ +0.85. This captures both sharp pullbacks AND stagnant consolidations while rejecting parabolic blow-offs (TCS Z5D = +3.1, HCLTECH Z5D = +3.4) and active crash waterfalls (Z5D < -2.60).
  • Pillar IV — Futures OI & Institutional Flow Guard: Rejects stocks undergoing aggressive Futures Short Buildup (5D Price Down + 5D Futures OI Surge > +3.5%) or severe backwardation.
